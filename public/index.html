03 / GOOD TO KNOW

Some answers.

Will the summary spoil the movie?

Only if you ask it to. Spoiler-free mode avoids twists, deaths and endings. Switch to Full story when you want everything.

Where does the film information come from?

Titles, cast, ratings and posters come from TMDB. The written analysis is generated by AI using only that verified information.

Do I need an account?

No. Summaries and recommendations work without one. Sign in with your email to save films to a watchlist that syncs across devices.

How do recommendations work?

Describe your mood, pick genres, choose an era and tell us what to avoid. We shortlist matching films and explain why each one fits.
